    <H1>Multi-Faith Devotionals</H1>
    <H1>Redemption</H1>
<UL>

<P>1) O Lord! Thou art the Remover of every anguish and the Dispeller of every affliction. Thou art He Who banisheth every sorrow and setteth free every slave, the Redeemer of every soul. O Lord! Grant deliverance through Thy mercy, and reckon me among such servants of Thine as have gained salvation. <BR><BR>
- A prayer by the Bab</P>

<P>2) He shall call to the heavens from above, and to the earth, that he may judge his people.<BR>
Gather my saints together unto me; those that have made a covenant with me by sacrifice.<BR>
And the heavens shall declare his righteousness: for God is judge himself. <BR><BR>
- Psalm 50: 4-6</P>

<P>3) Blessed is he who redeemed himself in My path and suffered tribulations for My name!<BR><BR>
– Bahá'u'lláh (Bahá'í Scriptures, p. 129)</P>

<P>4) No human being is so bad as to be beyond redemption.<BR><BR>
- Mahatma Ghandi</P>

<P>5) I think redemption is about righting a wrong, and in that pursuit it's about trying. You can stumble, you can make mistakes, but it's about trying to do the right thing.<BR><BR>
- Michael Jordan</P>

<P>6) He has delivered us from the domain of darkness and transferred us to the kingdom of his beloved Son, in whom we have redemption, the forgiveness of sins.<BR><BR>
- Colossians 1: 13-14</P>

<P>7) Mere improvement is not redemption, though redemption always improves people.<BR><BR>
- C.S. Lewis</P>

<P>8) I take pride in You, O my Darling Beloved. I am a sinner, continuously making mistakes; You are the Forgiving Lord.<BR><BR>
- Guru Granth Sahib (Sikhism)</P>

<P>9) Except for those who repent, believe and do righteous work. For them, Allah will replace their evil deeds with good. And ever is Allah Forgiving and Merciful.<BR><BR>
- Qur'an 25:70</P>

<P>10) Love has within it a redemptive power. And there is a power there that eventually transforms individuals.<BR><BR>
- Martin Luther King, Jr.</P>

<P>11) O Israel, hope in the Lord! For with the Lord there is steadfast love, and with him is plentiful redemption.<BR><BR>
- Psalm 130: 7</P>

<P>12) True redemption is... when guilt leads to good.<BR><BR>
- Khaled Hosseini</P>

<P>13) When a soul is thrown into darkness, sins will be committed. The guilty one is not he who commits the sin, but he who causes the darkness.<BR><BR>
- Victor Hugo <BR>
[Ryan's translation from French.]</P>

<P>14) Be victorious, and win the priceless jewel of this human life, by meditating on Him in the Saadh Sangat (the Company of the Holy), even for an instant.<BR><BR>
- Guru Granth Sahib (Sikhism)</P>

<P>15) Thou seest, O my Lord, how Thy servants are held captive by their own selves and desires. Redeem them from their bondage, O my God, by the power of Thy sovereignty and might... <BR><BR>
– Bahá'u'lláh (Bahá'í World Faith, p. 73)</P>

<P>16) Whoever among you commits evil ignorantly, then repents afterwards and mends their ways, then Allah is truly All-Forgiving, Most Merciful.<BR><BR>
- Qur'an 6:54</P>

<P>17) God is the ultimate musician. His music transforms your life. The notes of redemption rearrange your heart and restore your life. His songs of forgiveness, grace, reconciliation, truth, hope, sovereignty, and love give you back your humanity and restore your identity. <BR><BR>
- Paul David Tripp</P>

<P>18) Forgetfulness leads to exile, while remembrance is the secret of redemption.<BR><BR>
- Baal Shem Tov</P>

<P>19) Most of the heroes of Scripture rose to their greatest victories after their worst mistakes. God is in the redemption business, and He testifies of this through the lives of everyone He calls.<BR><BR>
- Rick Joyner</P>
