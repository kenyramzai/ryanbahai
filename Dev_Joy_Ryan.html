Multi-Faith Devotionals

Joy

    1) Send, therefore, unto my loved ones, O my God, what will cheer their hearts, and illumine their faces, and delight their souls. Thou knowest, O my Lord, that their joy is to behold the exaltation of Thy Cause and the glorification of Thy word. Do Thou unveil, therefore, O my God, what will gladden their eyes, and ordain for them the good of this world and of the world which is to come. Thou art, verily, the God of power, of strength and of bounty.

    - Prayer by Bahá’u’lláh

    2) May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, so that by the power of the Holy Spirit you may abound in hope.

    - Romans 15:13 (Bible)

    3) Count it all joy, my brothers, when you meet trials of various kinds,

    - James 1:2 (Bible)

    4) A joyful heart is good medicine, but a crushed spirit dries up the bones.

    - Proverbs 17:22 (Bible)

    5) Joy gives us wings! In times of joy our strength is more vital, our intellect keener, and our understanding less clouded. We seem better able to cope with the world and to find our sphere of usefulness.

    - ‘Abdu’l-Bahá, Paris Talks, p. 109

    6) Joy comes to us in ordinary moments. We risk missing out when we get too busy chasing down the extraordinary.

    - Brene' Brown

    7) Comparison is the thief of joy

    - Theodore Roosevelt

    8) Joy is the simplest form of gratitude.

    - Karl Barth

    9) A joy shared is a joy doubled.

    - Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

    10) Find ecstasy in life; the mere sense of living is joy enough.

    - Emily Dickinson

    11) The deepest sorrow carves into one's being, the more joy one can hold.

    - Khalil Gibran (from The Prophet)

    12) O SON OF MAN!
    Rejoice in the gladness of thine heart, that thou mayest be worthy to meet Me and to mirror forth My beauty.

    -Bahá’u’lláh, The Arabic Hidden Words

    13) ‘Abdu’l-Bahá wisheth no one's heart to feel hurt, nor will he be a source of grief to any one; for there is no greater satisfaction than being a source of joy to the hearts. I ask God that ye may become like angels of heaven, sources of happiness to souls.

    -‘Abdu’l-Bahá, Tablets of ‘Abdu’l-Bahá v1, p. 23
