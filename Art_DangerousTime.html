The most dangerous and crucial time in the Baha'i Faith

    I was always interested in Baha’i history – particularly crucial times of attacks from without and within. In most of these, the believers rallied around the appointed successor. The Babis rallied around Baha’u’llah, the believers later rallied around Abdu’l-Baha, and again the believers rallied around Shoghi Effendi, the Guardian of the Baha’i Faith. These were all crucial times, with attacks from within that were vicious and tested the believers. But there was a time even more dangerous and crucial, a time when the Faith was without leadership.

    During the ten year world crusade, launched by Shoghi Effendi, the Guardian, the Baha’i Faith went from about 40 countries to over 180 countries. Inspired by the Guardian, the Baha’i friends arose everywhere to go all over the planet. The end of the crusade was to be the election of the first Universal House of Justice, the crowning institution of the world order of Baha’u’llah. So during this crusade, many National Spiritual Assemblies saw their birth. And they were young.

    Well, something unexpected happened. 4 years into the world crusade, the Guardian, Shoghi Effendi, died in 1957. This was 6 years before the election of the Universal House of Justice. To make matters worse, the Guardian never had any children, so he never appointed another Guardian. According to the explicit text of Abdu’l-Baha’s will and testament, the Guardianship of the Baha’i Faith was to follow that blessed lineage, as Shoghi Effendi was related to both the Bab and Baha’u’llah. So Shoghi Effendi was to pass the Guardianship to his offspring.

    Seeing this void in the Baha’i leadership, the Hands of the Cause of God, appointed by Shoghi Effendi for protection and propagation of the Faith, got together in Haifa, Israel, in the Holy Land. They searched for Shoghi Effendi’s will, but they couldn’t find any. They then all, unanimously, signed a document saying there could not be another Guardian of the Baha’i Faith. They then procyeeded to take charge of the Baha’i Faith for the 6 years left of the world crusade and organized the election of the Universal House of Justice.

    This was a terrible time for the Baha’i world, most of which were new believers.

    In effect, one of the Hands of the Cause who had been one of Shoghi Effendi’s secretaries, arose and proclaimed himself the next Guardian of the Baha’i Faith. And members of one National Assembly, so young in Faith, were willing to follow him. His name was Mason Ramey. And he is the only Hand of the Cause who made a move for leadership.

    The other Hands of the Cause of God got together, and ex-communicated Mason Ramey from the Baha’i Faith. They also dissolved the National Assembly willing to follow him and appointed an administrative committee in its stead. They went even further and sent a Hand of the Cause to explain the situation everywhere.

    Once the Universal House of Justice was elected, the Hands of the Cause swore their allegiance to the House of Justice. They had even asked the voters not to elect any Hand of the Cause to the Supreme body. In return, the newly elected House of Justice thanked the Hands of the Cause for saving the Baha’i Faith. It was a mutual respect established between the House of Justice and the Hands of the Cause.

    One of the first issues the Universal House of Justice had to face was the issue of the Guardian. They announced that they could not appoint another Guardian.

    At this point, the enemies of the Faith stated that the Universal House of Justice could not be infallible because it was missing a Guardian. According to the will and testament of Abdu’l-Baha, the Guardian was to have a permanent seat of the House of Justice. Hence, these enemies argued that without a Guardian, the House of Justice lost some of what it should be.

    This issue personally bothered me for years. I knew in my heart, intuitively, that the House of Justice was infallible. And besides, I would ask, what choice did we have? There was no Guardian. We did the best we could. But I was longing for a counter-argument to this logic from the enemies of the Faith.

    After several years, I finally found my answer in the history of the Faith itself. Abdu’l-Baha, when his life was threatened by the Turkish government, following false allegations made by covenant breakers, wrote a Tablet to a believer named Haji Mirza Muhammad-Taqi. In this Tablet, Abdu’l-Baha orders him to organize the election of the House of Justice, should he die. So if that had happened, the House of Justice would have existed without a Guardian. That document is proof that the House of Justice’s infallibility is not based on having a Guardian as it’s member.

    A note about this same believer. Abdu’l-Baha had put him in charge of building the first Baha’i temple in the world, in Ishkabad. Unfortunately, many years after it was constructed, in 1963, there was an earthquake. And following this, the building was deemed too dangerous to be inside. So it was demolished.

    As to the Hands of the Cause of God and the role that they played, the history of mankind shows that people put in power by circumstances never give up that power. But these Hands of the Cause stayed true to the Faith, with that one exception, and they let go of the power as soon as they organized the election of the House of Justice.

    I will finish my article with a story of how Shoghi Effendi’s wife, Ruhiyyih Khanum Rabbani, “saved the day” after his death. The Hands of the Cause had gathered in front of the Shrine of Baha’u’llah, and they all had Writings from Shoghi Effendi. Well the Writings written to the east were very different from the Writings written to the west. So much so, that the Hands from each side of the world did not recognize the Writings from the other side. This is because Shoghi Effendi wrote differently to east and west. To the west he would give explanations and logic and the reasons why for things. Because this is what people in the west like. In the east, however, Shoghi Effendi would write like a king to his subjects. Because that is what people in the east like. So Shoghi Effendi’s wife got up and ended the argument saying “my husband wrote all of these.”

    As a last remark, I wish to quote Shoghi Effendi regarding the Guardian and the Universal House of Justice. “Neither can, nor will ever, infringe upon the sacred and prescribed domain of the other. Neither will seek to curtail the specific and undoubted authority with which both have been divinely invested.”

      Keny Ramzai
      April 2019

    Addition to Article

    I was surprised to find the following passage where Baha’u’llah predicted all the changes that were to take place in the leadership of the Baha’i Faith. The passage, found in the Kitab-i-Aqdas (Book of Laws) first appeared when quoted by the Universal House of Justice on December 7, 1969. It is actually a passage about the Baha’i tithing, called the Huququ’llah:

    " The endowments dedicated to charity revert to God, the Revealer of signs. No one has the right to lay hold on them without leave from the Dawning-place of Revelation. After Him the decision rests with the Aghsan (Branches), and after them with the House of Justice – should it be established in the world by then – so that they may use these endowments for the benefit of the Sites exalted in this Cause, and for that which they have been commanded by God, the Almighty, the All Powerful. Otherwise the endowments should be referred to the people of Baha, who speak not without His leave and who pass no judgement but in accordance with that which God has ordained in this Tablet, they who are the champions of victory betwixt heaven and earth, so that they may spend them on that which has been decreed in the Holy Book by God, the Mighty, the Bountiful. "

    Adib Taherzadeh explains the prediction historically, in “Revelation of Baha’u’llah” volume 3, chapter 17, Kitab-i-Aqdas 5., The Covenant of Baha’u’llah, p.373:

    " The word Aghsan (plural of Ghusn) literally means ‘Branches’. This word was exclusively used by Baha’u’llah to refer to His male descendants. In the above passage this word is used in the plural which indicates that there would be more than one Ghusn in His Dispensation who would become the Centre of the Cause. It turned out to be two, Abdu’l-Baha and Shoghi Effendi, the Guardian of the Faith. In this passage there is a striking indication that there will be a break in the line of the Aghsan. It also makes provision for the eventuality that this line might end before the House of Justice had been instituted – a situation which actually took place after the passing of Shoghi Effendi in 1957. For a little over five years the Hands of the Cause of God managed the affairs of the Baha’i community as custodians of the Faith, and in 1963 the Universal House of Justice came into being and took over the reins of the Cause of God. "
