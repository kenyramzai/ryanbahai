    <H1>Grit and Resilience and Perseverance</H1>
<UL>

<P>1) I can be changed by what happens to me. But I refuse to be reduced by it. <BR><BR>
— Maya Angelou</P>

<P>2) Enthusiasm is common. Endurance is rare.<BR><BR>
― Angela Duckworth</P>

<P>3) Resilience is accepting your new reality, even if it’s less good than the one you had before. You can fight it, you can do nothing but scream about what you’ve lost, or you can accept that and try to put together something that’s good.<BR><BR>
― Elizabeth Edwards</P>

<P>4) If your heart is broken, make art with the pieces.<BR><BR>
― Shane Koyczan</P>

<P>5) No matter how much falls on us, we keep plowing ahead. That’s the only way to keep the roads clear.<BR><BR>
― Greg Kincaid</P>

<P>6) My barn having burned down, I can now see the moon.<BR><BR>
― Mizuta Masahide (17th century Japanese poet and samurai)</P>

<P>7) You may have to fight a battle more than once to win it.<BR><BR>
― Margaret Thatcher</P>

<P>8) Do not judge me by my success, judge me by how many times I fell down and got back up again.<BR><BR>
― Nelson Mandela</P>

<P>9) She stood in the storm and when the wind did not blow her way, she adjusted her sails.<BR><BR>
― Elizabeth Edwards</P>

<P>10) On the other side of a storm is the strength that comes from having navigated through it. Raise your sail and begin.<BR><BR>
― Gregory S. Williams</P>

<P>11) Our greatest glory is not in never falling, but in rising every time we fall.<BR><BR>
― Confucius</P>

<P>12) We are not a product of what has happened to us in our past. We have the power of choice.<BR><BR>
― Stephen Covey</P>

<P>13) We are afflicted in every way, but not crushed; perplexed, but not driven to despair; persecuted, but not forsaken; struck down, but not destroyed.<BR><BR>
2 Corinthians 4: 8-9 (Bible)</P>

<P>14) Blessed is the man who remains steadfast under trial, for when he has stood the test he will receive the crown of life, which God has promised to those who love Him. <BR><BR>
- James 1:12 (Bible)</P>

<P>15) Allah does not burden a soul beyond that it can bear. <BR><BR>
- Qur'an 2:286</P>

<P>16) And We will surely test you with something of fear and hunger and a loss of wealth and lives and fruits, but give good tidings to the patient.<BR><BR>
- Qur'an 2:155</P>

<P>17) Victories are won usually through a great deal of patience, planning and perseverance, and rarely accomplished at a single stroke.<BR><BR>
- Shoghi Effendi, Lights of Guidance</P>

<P>18) Perseverance is an essential condition. In every project firmness and steadfastness will undoubtedly lead to good results; otherwise it will exist for some days, and then be discontinued.<BR><BR>
- ‘Abdu’l-Bahá, The Compilation of Compilations</P>

<P>19) Do not pray for an easy life, pray for the strength to endure a difficult one. <BR><BR>
— Bruce Lee</P>
