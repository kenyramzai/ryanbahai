Multi-Faith Devotionals

Generosity

    1) Generosity covers all faults.

    - French Proverb

    2) Generosity is extending the most generous interpretation possible to the intentions, words, and actions of others.

    - Brene' Brown

    3) Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.

    - 2 Corinthians 9:7

    4) O MY SERVANT!
    The best of men are they that earn a livelihood by their calling and spend upon themselves and upon their kindred for the love of God, the Lord of all worlds.

    - Baha'u'llah, the Hidden Words, Persian no. 82

    5) You shall never attain the (perfect standard) of righteousness until you spend (in charity) from (the wealth) that you love; and whatever you spend, Allāh surely knows it.
    (O believers) Spend from the good things which you have [earned] and which We have produced for you from the earth.

    - Surah Al-Baqarah: 263-273 (Qu'ran)

    6) One should train in three deeds of merit—generosity, a balanced life, developing a loving mind—that yield long-lasting happiness.

    — The Buddha (from the Itivuttika)

    7) Whoever is generous to the poor lends to the LORD, and he will reward them for what they have done.

    - Proverbs 19:17

    8) Charity purifies even the great souls.

    – Lord Krishna (Bhagavad Gita, Chapter 18, Verse 5)

    9) Be kind to all beings - this is more meritorious than bathing at the sixty-eight sacred shrines of pilgrimage and the giving of charity.

    - Guru Amar Das (Sikhism)

    10) O CHILDREN OF DUST!
    Tell the rich of the midnight sighing of the poor, lest heedlessness lead them into the path of destruction, and deprive them of the Tree of Wealth. To give and to be generous are attributes of Mine; well is it with him that adorneth himself with My virtues.

    - Baha'u'llah, the Hidden Words, Arabic no. 49

    11) A generous person will prosper; whoever refreshes others will be refreshed.

    - Proverbs 11:25

    12) Don't feel ashamed if the amount of charity is small because to refuse the needy is an act of greater shame.

    - Imam Ali (Islam)

    13) Give, even if you only have a little.

    — The Buddha (from the Dhammapada)

    14) That gift which is given as a matter of duty, without expectation of return, at the right place and time, to a worthy person—such charity is considered to be in the mode of goodness.

    – Lord Krishna (Bhagavad Gita Chapter 17, Verse 20)

    15) Be generous in prosperity, and thankful in adversity.

    - Beginning of the Tablet of the Just by Baha'u'llah

    16) Baha'u'llah says it is not charity unless you give away the things you hold dear.

    - Mulla Rida
