MULTI FAITH DEVOTIONAL
KIDNESS

1) A man who is kind benefits himself, but a cruel man hurts himself.
- Proverbs 11:17

2) Kindness is the language which the deaf can hear and the blind can see. 
— Mark Twain

3) Carry out a random act of kindness, with no expectation of reward, safe in the knowledge that one day, someone might do the same for you.
— Princess Diana

4) No act of kindness, no matter how small, is ever wasted.
- Aesop

5) Be kind, for everyone you meet is fighting a battle you know nothing about.
- Plato

6) What do we live for, if it is not to make life less difficult for each other?
- George Eliot

7) Be kind whenever possible. It is always possible.
- Dalai Lama

8) Generosity involves assuming the best about others' intentions and actions.
- Brene Brown

*** INTERLUDE: Christie singing "Ave Maria"

9) Educate the children in their infancy in such a way that they may become exceedingly kind and merciful to the animals. If an animal is sick they should endeavor to cure it; if it is hungry, they should feed it; if it is thirsty, they should satisfy its thirst; if it is tired, they should give it rest.
- Abdu'l Baha

10) Even as a mother protects with her life her child, her only child, so with a boundless heart should one cherish all living beings. Radiating kindness over the entire world... unbounded, freed from hatred and ill-will.
— The Buddha

11) Be kind to all beings, this is more meritorious than bathing at the sixty-eight sacred shrines of pilgrimage and donating money. 
— Guru Nanak (Sikhism)

12) Kind words and forgiveness are better than charity followed by injury. And Allah is Self-Sufficient, Most Forbearing. 
- Quran 2:263

13) And be kind to parents, relatives, orphans, the poor, near and distant neighbors, close friends, [needy] travelers...
- Quran 4:36

14) Do not be content with showing friendship in words alone, let your heart burn with loving kindness for all who may cross your path.
— Bahá'u'lláh

15) Be ye sincerely kind, not in appearance only. Let each one of God's loved ones centre his attention on this: to be the Lord's mercy to man... Let him do some good to every person whose path he crosseth. 
— ʻAbdu'l-Baha
