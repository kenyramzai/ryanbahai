    <H1>Ryan's Baha'i Site</H1>
    <H1>Multi-Faith Devotionals</H1>
    <H1>Fellowship</H1>
<UL>


<P>1) But if we walk in the light, as he is in the light, we have fellowship one with another, and the blood of Jesus Christ his Son cleanseth us from all sin.<BR><BR>
- 1 John 1:7 (Bible)</P>

<P>2) And let us consider one another to provoke unto love and to good works:<BR>
Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as the manner of some is; but exhorting one another: and so much the more, as ye see the day approaching.<BR><BR>
- Hebrews 10:24-25 (Bible)</P>

<P>3) The believers are but one brotherhood, so make peace between your brothers. And be mindful of Allah so you may be shown mercy.<BR><BR>
- Surah Al-Hujurat 49:10 (Qur'an)</P>

<P>4) ...And [He] brought together their hearts. If you had spent all that is in the earth, you could not have brought their hearts together; but Allah brought them together..<BR><BR>
- Surah Al-Anfal 8:63 (Qur'an)</P>

<P>5) No one is my enemy, and none is a stranger to me. Everyone is my friend.<BR><BR>
- Guru Arjan Dev Ji (Sikhism)</P>

<P>6) Friend, if you possess some good, let us be friends. Let us be partners for doing good, and let us ignore each other's flaws.<BR><BR>
- Guru Nanak Dev Ji (Sikhism)</P>

<P>7) ... This is the entire holy life, ... that is, good friendship, good companionship, good comradeship.<BR><BR>
- The Buddha</P>

<P>8) Just as the dawn is the forerunner of the sunrise, so good friendship is the forerunner for the arising of the noble eightfold path.<BR><BR>
- The Buddha</P>

<P>9) Consort with the followers of all religions in a spirit of friendliness and fellowship.<BR><BR>
- Baha'u'llah</P>

<P>10) With the utmost friendliness and in a spirit of perfect fellowship take ye counsel together, and dedicate the precious days of your lives to the betterment of the world and the promotion of the Cause of Him Who is the Ancient and Sovereign Lord of all.<BR><BR>
- Baha'u'llah</P>

<P>11) But ye, brethren, be not weary in well doing.<BR>
And if any man obey not our word by this epistle, note that man, and have no company with him, that he may be ashamed.<BR>
Yet count him not as an enemy, but admonish him as a brother.<BR><BR>
- 2 Thessalonians 3:13-15 (Bible)</P>
    
</UL>
