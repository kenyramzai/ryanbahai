Multi-Faith Devotionals

Wellness

    1) Physical fitness is not only one of the most important keys to a healthy body; it is the basis of dynamic and creative intellectual activity.

    – John F. Kennedy

    2) Those who do not find time for exercise will have to find time for illness.

    – Earl of Derby

    3) It is remarkable how one’s wits are sharpened by physical exercise.

    – Pliny the Younger

    4) It is exercise alone that supports the spirit, and keeps the mind in vigor.

    – Cicero

    5) The mind is the most important part of achieving any fitness goal. Mental change always comes before physical change.

    – Matt McGorry

    6) If we could give every individual the right amount of nourishment and exercise, not too little and not too much, we would have found the safest way to health.

    – Hippocrates

    7) Walking is cheap, fun, safe and acceptable exercise.

    – Annie Taylor

    8) Reading is to the mind what exercise is to the body.

    – Joseph Addison

    9) People say that losing weight is no walk in the park. When I hear that I think, yeah, that’s the problem.

    – Chris Adams

    10) To lead a noble life, first protect your greatest asset, your mind.

    - The daily Buddhist: 366 days of wisdom for happiness, inner freedom, and mindful living. (By Pema Sherpa and Brandon Barca)

    11) Remember, in every situation, no matter how black and white it may appear, there is always more than meets the eye.

    - Same source as previous

    12) Seven times a day do I praise thee because of thy righteous judgments.
    Great peace have they which love thy law: and nothing shall offend them.
    LORD, I have hoped for thy salvation, and done thy commandments.

    - Psalms 119:164-166 (Torah)

    13) My tongue shall speak of thy word: for all thy commandments are righteousness.
    Let thine hand help me; for I have chosen thy precepts.

    - Psalms 119:172-173
