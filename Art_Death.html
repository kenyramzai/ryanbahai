Some Baha'i Perspectives on Life After Death

    One of our goals in this life is to prepare ourselves spiritually for the next life.

    When we die, our body returns to the material world and our soul returns to the worlds of God.

    The souls of people who believe in God can communicate with each other.

    Also, Baha’u’llah says that if someone is a believer, all their ancestors are forgiven in the next life. (Your parents, grandparents, great grandparents, and so on…)

    There is also a passage that says the souls of believers will be aware of other people’s actual spiritual rank in the next life.

    Still, another passage says that all knowledge acquired goes with us to the next life and is not lost. This is how scientists who are pure of heart here are influenced by scientists who have died, and influenced into making discoveries. Likewise, the love we have for others also carries on to the next life, just like knowledge.

    Another feature is that people in the next life see us, even though we don’t see them. It’s like a baby in the stomach whom everybody sees but who doesn’t see us.

    Also, people are closer or further away from God depending on what they did. So there are as many heavens and hells as there are individuals.

    The two worlds are not separate, but connected. This is how some people feel the presence of someone in the next life.

    Then, the souls of believers of the past are assigned as guardians to watch over the souls of believers in the present.

    Also, it is stated we will progress towards God for eternity.

    The reason death is a mystery is that some people would kill themselves to go there if they knew how cool it was. Others would be filled with sorrow because of what they’ve done.

    As far as what we can do to help the souls of those who died, we can not only pray for them, but we can also perform good actions in their name.

    Finally, in the Hidden Words, it says that only our good actions of untouched purity will be accepted at the time of death. That will be our luggage.

    Of course, money and material stuff do not go with you.

      Keny Ramzai
      August 2019

    Addition to Article

    Although quotes abound in the Faith on the topic, I have selected only 2 of them to add to this article.

    “Sorrow not if, in these days and on this earthly plane, things contrary to your wishes have been ordained and manifested by God, for days of blissful joy, of heavenly delight, are assuredly in store for you. Worlds, holy and spiritually glorious, will be unveiled to your eyes. You are destined by Him, in this world and hereafter, to partake of their benefits, to share in their joys, and to obtain a portion of their sustaining grace.”
    – Baha’u’llah (Gleanings from the Writings of Baha’u’llah)

    “As the spirit of man after putting off this material form has an everlasting life, certainly any existing being is capable of making progress; Therefore it is permitted to ask for advancement, forgiveness, mercy, beneficence, and blessings for a man after his death, because existence is capable of progression. That is why in the prayers of Baha’u’llah forgiveness and remission of sins are asked for those who have died.”
    – Abdu’l-Baha (Baha’i World Faith, p. 329)

      Keny Ramzai
      October 2020
