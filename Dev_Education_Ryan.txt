MULTI FAITH PRAYER SESSION
EDUCATION

1) All Creation is used by God through His Word to deliver and provide for His chosen people.
- Portals of Prayer (Lutheran)

2) The Word of God does not sit idly waiting for a curious passerby to flip through the pages of an old Bible in a book shop or a grandmother's table. It sparks life and joy in the heart of the repentant, and fear or conviction in the heart of the arrogant. It comforts, confuses, convicts and collects. It is the method by which the nations are brought under Christ's gracious reign, the route by which the lost are found, the guilty redeemed, the convicted made clean.
- Portals of Prayer (Lutheran)

3) The source of all learning is the knowledge of God, exalted be His Glory, and this cannot be attained save through the knowledge of His Divine Manifestation.
- Bahá’u’lláh, Tablets of Bahá’u’lláh, p. 156

4) He promulgated the adoption of the same course of education for man and woman. Daughters and sons must follow the same curriculum of study, thereby promoting unity of the sexes. When all mankind shall receive the same opportunity of education and the equality of men and women be realized, the foundations of war will be utterly destroyed. Without equality this will be impossible because all differences and distinction are conducive to discord and strife.
- ‘Abdu’l-Bahá, The Promulgation of Universal Peace, p. 175

5) Jesus Christ was an Educator of humanity. His teachings were altruistic; His bestowal, universal. He taught mankind by the power of the Holy Spirit and not through human agency, for the human power is limited, whereas the divine power is illimitable and infinite. The influence and accomplishment of Christ will attest this.
- ‘Abdu’l-Bahá, The Promulgation of Universal Peace, p. 85

6) The difference between spiritual philosophers and others is shown by their lives. The spiritual teacher shows his belief in his own teaching by himself being what he recommends to others..the life and morals of a spiritual man are, in themselves, an education to those who know him.
- Compilations, Quickeners of Mankind, p. 120

7) INTERLUDE: Christie singing Amazing Grace

8) Allah will raise those who have believed among you and those who were given knowledge by degrees. 
- Qur'an 58:11

9) Education is our passport to the future, for tomorrow belongs to the people who prepare for it today.
- Malcom X

10) It does not matter how slowly you go as long as you do not stop.
- Confucius

11) Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world.
- Nelson Mandela

12) The beautiful thing about learning is that no one can take it away from you.
- B. B. King

13) All our dreams can come true if we have the courage to pursue them. 
- Walt Disney
