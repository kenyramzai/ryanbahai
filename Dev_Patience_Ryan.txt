MULTI FAITH DEVOTIONAL
PATIENCE

1) O SON OF MAN! 
For everything there is a sign. The sign of love is fortitude under My decree and patience under My trials.
- The Hidden Words, Arabic, No. 48 (Baha'u'llah)

2) Patience is not passive, on the contrary it is concentrated strength. 
- Bruce Lee

3) He that can have patience can have what he will 
- Benjamin Franklin

4) Patience is bitter, but the fruit is sweet 
- Aristotle

5) The two most powerful warriors are patience and time
- Leo Tolstoy

6) Trees that are slow to grow bear the best fruit 
- Moliere

7) Rivers know this: there is no hurry. We shall get there some day.
- A.A. Milne

8) How poor are they that have not patience! What wound did ever heal but by degrees?
- Shakespeare (Othello)

*** INTERLUDE - CHRISTIE SINGING "Steal Away"

9) We must accept finite disappointment, but never lose infinite hope
- Martin Luther King Jr.

10) Patience and time do more than strength or passion
- Jean de la Fontaine

11) Trust in God's timing. It's better to wait a while and have things fall in place than to rush and have things fall apart. 

12) Patience with others is love, patience with self is hope, patience with God is Faith. 
- Adel Bestavros

13) The most important things are learned over time, not over night. 

14) The time is always right to do what is right.
- Martin Luther King Jr.

15) I had fainted, unless I had believed to see the goodness of the Lord in the land of the living.
Wait on the Lord: be of good courage, and he shall strengthen thine heart: wait, I say, on the Lord.
- Psalm 27: 13-14
