MULTI FAITH DEVOTIONAL
PEACE

1) Rejoice in the Lord always: and again I say, Rejoice.
Let your moderation be known unto all men. The Lord is at hand.
Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God.
And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.
Finally, brethren, whatsoever things are true, whatsoever things are honest, whatsoever things are just, whatsoever things are pure, whatsoever things are lovely, whatsoever things are of good report; if there be any virtue, and if there be any praise, think on these things.
Those things, which ye have both learned, and received, and heard, and seen in me, do: and the God of peace shall be with you.
- Bible: Philippians 4: 4-9

2) Peace comes from within. Do not seek it without. 
— Buddha

3) Peace cannot be kept by force. It can only be achieved by understanding. 
— Albert Einstein

4) True peace is not merely the absence of tension: it is the presence of justice. 
— Martin Luther King Jr.

5) There is no 'way to peace,' there is only 'peace'." — Mahatma Gandhi

6) The well-being of mankind, its peace and security, are unattainable unless and until its unity is firmly established.
- Baha'u'llah, Gleanings CXXXI

7) You will find peace not by trying to escape your problems, but by confronting them courageously.
- J. Donald Walters

*** INTERLUDE: Christie singing "Peace like a river."

8) Peace isn't a feeling to relate to. It's a higher quality of beingness to return to. 
- John de Ruiter

9) There was never a good war or a bad peace. 
— Benjamin Franklin

10) When the power of love overcomes the love of power, the world will know peace.
― Jimi Hendrix (Ghandi also said this)

11) Thou hast made us for thyself, O Lord, and our heart is restless until it finds its rest in thee.
― Augustine of Hippo

12) Darkness cannot drive out darkness: only light can do that. Hate cannot drive out hate: only love can do that.
― Martin Luther King Jr.

13) True peace and tranquillity will only be realized when every soul will have become the well-wisher of all mankind. 
— Bahá'u'lláh

14) Peace begins with a smile. 
— Mother Teresa
