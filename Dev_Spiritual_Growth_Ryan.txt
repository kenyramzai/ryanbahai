    <H1>Spiritual Growth</H1>
<UL>

<P>1) O taste and see that the Lord is good: blessed is the man that trusteth in him.<BR><BR>
- Psalm 34: 8</P>

<P>2) The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.<BR>
Many are the afflictions of the righteous: but the Lord delivereth him out of them all.<BR>
He keepeth all his bones: not one of them is broken.<BR><BR>
- Psalm 34: 18-20</P>

<P>3) The greatest struggle is with your own soul.<BR><BR>
- Hadith (saying)</P>

<P>4) True wealth isn't having a lot. It's being content.<BR><BR>
- Prophet Muhammad</P>

<P>5) Do not dwell in the past. Do not dream of the future. Concentrate the mind on the present moment.<BR><BR>
- Buddha</P>

<P>6) Be where you are; otherwise you will miss your life.<BR><BR>
- Buddha</P>

<P>7) There are no problems, only opportunities for growth.<BR><BR>
- Rebbetzin Dena Weinberg</P>

<P>8) One question is always relevant: How can I use this to move forward?<BR><BR>
- Rebbetzin Tziporah Heller</P>

<P>9) If you believe breaking is possible, believe fixing is possible.<BR><BR>
- Rabbi Nachman of Breslov</P>

<P>10) The Beatitudes<BR><BR>

Blessed are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.<BR>
Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted.<BR>
Blessed are the meek: for they shall inherit the earth.<BR>
Blessed are they which do hunger and thirst after righteousness: for they shall be filled.<BR>
Blessed are the merciful: for they shall obtain mercy.<BR>
Blessed are the pure in heart: for they shall see God.<BR>
Blessed are the peacemakers: for they shall be called the children of God.<BR>
Blessed are they which are persecuted for righteousness' sake: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.<BR><BR>
- Matthew 5: 3-10</P>

<P>11) Lord! Thou seest him that kneeleth yearning to arise and serve Thee, the dead calling for eternal life from the ocean of Thy favor and craving to soar to the heavens of Thy wealth, the stranger longing for his home of glory ‘neath the canopy of Thy grace, the seeker hastening by Thy mercy to Thy door of bounty, the sinful turning to the ocean of forgiveness and pardon.<BR><BR>
- Part of a Baha'i Prayer for Spiritual Growth</P>
