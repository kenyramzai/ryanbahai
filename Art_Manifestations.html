Time between a Manifestation of God receiving Revelation and revealing Himself

    The Founders of the world's great Religions are called "Manifestations of God" by Baha'is, which implies "an image of God on earth", among many other things.

    We know that they all received from God the news of who they were, Messiahs who had great spiritual missions of reviving and improving the entire world, starting at the grassroots with individuals.

    There is a time difference between when a Manifestation received the news of their mission, and when they chose to announce their mission for the first time.

    Moses received news of His mission through the burning bush, Jesus described it as a dove from heaven, Muhammad had taken refuge in a cave where a spider spoke to Him of the mission, and Baha'u'llah received intimation of His station and mission through a vision of a maid from heaven.

    We do not know exactly how the Bab and Buddha received Revelation of their mission.

    In the case of the Bab, there was one episode where his wife found Him on the roof of their house in such a state that could be described as communing with God. But was that the exact moment when he found out about His mission? [I have no idea.]

    We do know the exact night that He revealed Himself to His first Apostle, who had found Him on his (apostle) own.

    In the case of Buddha, we know that he spent 9 years of isolation in a forest before coming back to His fellow human beings and preaching the value of virtues - going so far as saying that the pursuit of virtues is indeed the pursuit of happiness. Does this mean that Buddha waited 9 years before announcing His mission? [It's not a bad conclusion but again there is no knowledge either way.]

    In the case of Moses, we know the time that passed by between the burning bush and when He revealed Himself.

    Baha'u'llah waited 19 entire years to reveal who He was to the world.

    In the case of Muhammad, he had to wait the longest, 40 years, before telling the world. Why? Because the culture He was in only listened to older people.

    Jesus Christ explained His mission fully first to His Apostles on the Mount. But we still have no idea exactly how many years passed between His baptism in the water by John the Baptist - meaning the exact moment He got the message from the Dove of Heaven - and when He revealed His mission on the Mount to the Apostles.

    So we know how much time went by between receiving intimation from God of their missions and the exact moment of declaration to the world of who They are, in the cases of Moses, Muhammad, and Baha'u'llah.

    We do NOT know in the cases of Buddha, the Bab and Jesus.

    These Manifestations need so many years to digest their intimate connection with God and also plan out how to reveal to the world who They are and go about Their plan of action - knowing they will be the subject of heavy persecutions and attacks.

      - Keny Ramzai (pen name for Ryan Kazemi)
      April 2022
