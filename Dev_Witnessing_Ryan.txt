    <H1>Multi-Faith Devotionals</H1>
    <H1>Witnessing (Leading by example)</H1>
<UL>

<P>1) When we mortgage the present for the future, we are not only asking for depression in the future, we are also deadening ourselves to the present.<BR><BR>
- Eknath Easwaran (The Mantram Handbook)</P>

<P>2) In the same way, let your light shine before others, that they may see your good deeds and glorify your Father in heaven.<BR><BR>
- Matthew 5:18 (Bible)</P>

<P>3) Always be prepared to give an answer to everyone who asks you to give the reason for the hope that you have. But do this with gentleness and respect.<BR><BR>
- 1 Peter 3:15 (Bible)</P>

<P>4) O you who have believed, be persistently standing firm in justice, witnesses for Allah, even if it be against yourselves or parents and relatives. Whether one is rich or poor, Allah is more worthy of both. So follow not [personal] inclination, lest you not be just.<BR><BR>
- Surah An-Nisa 4:135 (Qur'an)</P>

<P>5) Our Lord, we have believed in what You revealed and have followed the messenger, so register us among the witnesses [to truth].<BR><BR>
- Surah Ali 'Imran 3:53 (Qur'an)</P>

<P>6) You shall not bear false witness against your neighbor.<BR><BR>
- Exodus 20:16 (Torah)</P>

<P>7) Conquer yourself is more valuable than winning a thousand battles, leading to an undeniable victory.<BR><BR>
- Buddhism</P>

<P>8) You can evaluate the quality of your authority by looking deeply to see if compassion is the foundation of your leadership.<BR><BR>
- Thích Nhất Hạnh (Buddhism)</P>

<P>9) Truth is the highest virtue, but higher still is truthful living.<BR><BR>
- Guru Nanak Dev Ji (Sikhism)</P>

<P>10) Let deeds, not words, be your adorning.<BR><BR>
- Baha'u'llah</P>

<P>11) The betterment of the world can be accomplished through pure and goodly deeds, through commendable and seemly conduct.<BR><BR>
- Baha'u'llah</P>

<P>12) The ultimate measure of a man is not where he stands in moments of comfort and convenience, but where he stands at times of challenge and controversy.<BR><BR>
- Rev Martin Luther King Jr.</P>

<P>13) Everybody can be great because everybody can serve... You only need a heart full of grace. A soul generated by love.<BR><BR>
- Rev Martin Luther King Jr.</P>

<P>14) For it is not preaching and rules the world wants, but love and action.<BR><BR>
- Shoghi Effendi (Guardian of the Baha'i Faith)</P>
