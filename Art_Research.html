Research into the Baha'i world 1983-1992
(Time period when the Faith doubled in size)

    The year my mother died, 1983, was the year the Seat of the House of Justice was built, on mount Carmel, Haifa, Israel. 10 years later, they started the Arc projects in the mountain. These projects added much needed buildings to the world center. Also, the beautification of the Baha’i lands, with terraces expending from the top to bottom of the holy mountain. They are still working on the Arc this very day.

    The temple in India was built, in the shape of a lotus flower. And T.V. stations around the world, including China and Russia, aired shows about it.

    The persecution of Baha’is in Iran was intense. And the Baha’i community launched a world wide campaign to bring this to the attention of governments around the world, and in the process presented the Faith to them. To give an idea of what transpired, 10 women ages 18 to 25 were hanged, for the crime of teaching Baha’i children who had been kicked out of school (because they were Baha’i). Some 500 Baha’is were thrown into prison. Many Baha’is found themselves homeless after their homes were confiscated. The house of Baha’u’llah was raised to the ground. Many Baha’is lost their jobs and others were kicked out of university. Harassment of Baha’i children continued. Inspired by all this, the Baha’i community brought these sacrifices to the whole world’s attention.

    One of the exciting developments of that time was the House of Justice calling the Baha’i world to launch the first socio-economic programs all over the world. In the very first year, 737 programs were launched by a very enthusiastic Baha’i community. These first social and economic programs were: tutorial schools, alphabetization, emancipation of women, agriculture programs, medical campaigns, the establishment of 7 radio stations (including one in South Carolina in the U.S.A.), the multiplication of Baha’i schools and Training Institutes.

    Also, the world center published “the Promise of World Peace” which was given to all governments and all people of capacity of the world. As a direct result, peace became the spirit of the end of the century. Later the world center published a document on the environment.

    Following the riots in Los Angeles, the National Spiritual Assembly of the Baha’is of the United States published the statement on race unity.

    During 1992, two important things took place. The first was the 2nd Baha’i world congress, held in New York. (I was 17 and I attended). It was the biggest gathering of Baha’is ever seen. The second was the publication of Baha’u’llah’s book of laws, the Kitab-i-Aqdas, into English. And, along with it, the application of the law of Huququ’llah (Baha’i tithing) to everyone.

    Last but not least, the Berlin wall fell, and the eastern block opened to religion. We sent a ton of pioneers and travel teachers with extremely good results. The populations were thirsty for God.

    During all this time, Baha’i literature was translated into 500 languages.

    As a result of all of this, particularly the persecution of Baha’is, the Baha’i community doubled from 2 million to 4 million.

      Keny Ramzai
      June 2018
