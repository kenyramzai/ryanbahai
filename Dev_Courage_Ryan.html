Multi-Faith Devotionals

Courage

    1) Courage is showing up and letting ourselves be seen, especially when we cannot control the outcome.

    - Brene' Brown (motivational speaker)

    2) The source of courage and power is the promotion of the Word of God, and steadfastness in His Love.

    - Bahá’u’lláh, Tablets of Bahá’u’lláh, p. 155

    3) Whatever decreaseth fear increaseth courage.

    - Bahá’u’lláh, Epistle to the Son of the Wolf, p. 32

    4) Fear not, for I am with you;
    be not dismayed, for I am your God;
    I will uphold you with my righteous right hand.

    - Isaiah 41:10

    5) When I am afraid,
    I put my trust in you.
    In God, whose word I praise,
    in God I trust; I shall not be afraid.

    - Psalm 56: 3-4

    6) The LORD is my light and my salvation;
    whom shall I fear?
    The LORD is the stronghold of my life;
    of whom shall I be afraid?

    - Psalm 27: 1

    7) It is in great dangers that we see great courage.

    - Epictetus (Stoic philosopher)

    8) I'll tell you what bravery really is. Bravery is just the determination to do a job that you know has to be done.

    - Audie Murphy (most decorated soldier in world war II)

    9) If your dreams don’t scare you, they’re not big enough.

    - Ellen Johnson Sirleaf

    10) Don’t give into your fears, if you do, you won’t be able to talk to your heart.

    - Paulo Coelho

    11) You can choose courage, or you can choose comfort, but you cannot choose both.

    - Brene Brown

    12) Life is a daring adventure or nothing

    - Helen Keller

    13) A ship in harbor is safe, but that’s not what ships are built for.

    - G.T. Shedd

    14) I learned that courage was not the absence of fear, but the triumph over it. The brave man is not he who does not feel afraid, but he who conquers that fear.

    - Nelson Mandela

    15) If you can’t fly, then run. If you can’t run, then walk. If you can’t walk, then crawl; but whatever you do, you have to keep moving forward.

    - Martin Luther King, Jr.
