MULTI FAITH DEVOTIONAL
CHILDREN OF GOD

1) Shoghi Effendi was chosen at 9 years old to be the next leader of the Baha'i Faith. Of course, this was unknown to the child. One of his teachers, Haji Mirza Haydar Ali, wrote to Abdu'l-Baha, "The child is not a fighter. How can it be him?" Abdu'l-Baha replied "If you look into the deep, sad eyes of my grandson, you will see the loyalty that he has for the Blessed Beauty."

2) According to the story of Jesus in the Qu'ran, the three wise men traveled for some time and when they found Mary and the child, they had a doubt as to whether or not that was the child they had been searching for. The child was too young to speak. But Mary told him "Defend yourself!" And Jesus as a toddler got up and made a whole speech!

3) Ali was 11 years old when he recognized Muhammad as the Prophet. He was actually the first human being to do so. But as he was a child, others disregarded him as the first. 

4) When Ali was 13 years old, he received an arrow in the ankle during a battle. He would yell as soon as someone touched the arrow. They came to see Prophet Muhammad to ask him what to do. Muhammad told them, "You see that child? Remove the arrow while he is praying." Sure enough, while Ali was praying, they removed the arrow and he was so much into his prayer he did not feel anything.

5) Rahula, Bouddha's son, inherited the spiritual kingdom as a child, becoming the first monk.

6) One of the early disciples who attained enlightenment in Buddhism was Sopaka, a child of 7 years who was orphaned and abandoned by his step father. 

7) In Sikhism, Har Krishan Ji became a Guru at 5 years of age.

8) Samuel, one of the prophets of Israel, followed the path since a very young age.

9) Miriam, Moses' sister who was very young, watched over Moses and took care of him and kept him safe.

10) Josiah became king of Judah at 8 years old, and turned the Nation back to following God's Law.

11) David as a mere boy defeated Goliath.

12) One of the early teachers of the Baha'i Faith was child by the name of Ruh'u'llah. He would look into a person's eyes and silently ask them if they were a Baha'i. Then he would get a silent answer and proceed from there.

13) When Baha'u'llah was a child of young years, a very unfair and unjust tax collector came to harass his family. Baha'u'llah disappeared for almost a week. The family was worried. He rode a horse for three days straight to reach the capital city of Teheran. The authorities were shocked that a mere child had undertaken such a journey through bandit infested roads. They immediately gave Baha'u'llah the letter saying that the tax collector was fired, which in turn Baha'u'llah returned home and handed the letter to his father.
