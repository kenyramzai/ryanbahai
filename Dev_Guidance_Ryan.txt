MULTI FAITH DEVOTIONAL
GUIDANCE

1) Wait for the Lord; Be strong, and let your heart take courage; Wait on the Lord!
- Psalm 27:14 (Bible)

2) To walk safely through the maze of human life, one needs the light of wisdom and the guidance of virtue.
- Gautama Buddha

3) With your hands carve out your own destiny.
- Guru Nanak (Sikhism)

4) There is a deep need in the world just now for guidance - almost any sort of spiritual guidance.
- Carl Jung

5) Spiritual guidance needs guidance. It's like comparing walking on the ground and mountain climbing. Once you learn how to walk, you can walk on the ground by yourself, but if you want to climb Mount Everest, you need a guide.
- Seyyed Hossein Nasr

6) IF Allah guides a person through you, it is better for you than all that is on the earth. 
- Hadith (Sarih El Boukhari)

7) Trust in the Lord with all your heart, And lean not on your own understanding; In all your ways acknowledge Him, And He shall direct your paths.
- Proverbs 3:5-6 (Bible)

8) Man says "Show me and I'll trust you". God says "Trust Me, and I'll show you".
- Psalm 126:6 (Bible)

9) Let your acts be a guide unto all mankind, for the professions of most men, be they high or low, differ from their conduct.
- Shoghi Effendi (The Advent of Divine Justice)

10) You are where you are, not by accident, but by the Will of your Creator, for your own development and for that of those around you.
- Abdu'l-Baha (Star of the West)
