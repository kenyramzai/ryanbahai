5 Things that the Baha'i Faith is to me

    Although I am passionate about my Faith, I have friends from many different religions and backgrounds, and they are passionate about their beliefs as well. The thing is that we all learn about each other’s Faiths.

    The Baha’i Faith is nothing short of the fight to save the planet, and to do this we are racing the clock. The world is gradually heading into a bigger and bigger conflict, an unavoidable future world war 3. And the Baha’i Faith is racing to unite the world and eliminate war. The Faith aims to unite races, religions, nations, etc. Unfortunately, it seems, sadly, that both will happen. We will have a devastating world war 3, and the Baha’i Faith will keep spreading during the war, and after all the horrors, the survivors will want a united world and an end to war.

    The Baha’i Faith is also like reaching university. In religions of the past, they only had 1 Holy Book. Baha’u’llah wrote the equivalent of 100 volumes of encyclopedia. Abdu’l-Baha also wrote about 100 volumes. Shoghi Effendi, the Guardian, then wrote more than 4000 letters in his life, clarifying things. Some of these letters are long enough they are printed as books. So in the Baha’i Faith, you can pick any subject of study and find stuff about it in many different places. It’s like reaching university in religion.

    The Baha’i Faith is the coming of the Kingdom of Heaven. This Kingdom has been promised by all religions. The time of prophecy is over. Now begins the time of accomplishment (of the prophecies). We are slowly but gradually bringing the Kingdom of Heaven to earth. The process will last maybe 500,000 years. But we’re starting the process now. The golden age of mankind is not in the past. The golden age of humanity is in the future.

    The Baha’i Faith is nothing short of a fantastic love story. It is the story of God not abandoning us and sending His message to us every 1000 years, to guide us and out of love for us. It is also the love story between God and His mysteriously chosen Mouthpiece, His Messenger. It is the love of this Messenger for God that makes Him accept all sorts of tribulations. It is in turn this Being of Manifestation’s love story between Him and those who accept and follow Him. This love causes thousands to lose their lives, and thousands to head for unknown destinations all over the world. The Faith is nothing but a love story.

    Finally, the Baha’i Faith is not a path of force. If the Faith was a path of force, it would not be the truth. If it said, “you have to be a Baha’i” or “this is the only way to be saved”, it would not be the truth, because there would be an element of force in accepting it. You are free to join. You are free to leave when you want to. You are free to come back to it later, leave again later, and so on.

      Keny Ramzai
      January 2020
